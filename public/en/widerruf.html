To meet the withdrawal deadline, it is sufficient for you to send the communication concerning your exercise of the right of withdrawal before the withdrawal period has expired.

Effects of withdrawal

If you withdraw from this contract, we shall reimburse all payments received from you without undue delay and in any event not later than fourteen days from the day on which we are informed about your decision to withdraw. We will carry out the reimbursement using the same means of payment as you used for the initial transaction, unless you have expressly agreed otherwise. In any event, you will not incur any fees as a result of the reimbursement.

Early expiry

The right of withdrawal expires early for a contract for services if we have fully performed the service and began performance only after you gave your express consent and at the same time confirmed that you knew you would lose the right of withdrawal once the contract had been fully performed (§ 18 (1) item 1 FAGG).

For digital services not supplied on a tangible medium, the right of withdrawal also expires if you expressly agreed that we begin performance before the end of the withdrawal period and you acknowledged that you lose the right of withdrawal once performance begins (§ 18 (1) item 11 FAGG).

Lumen Pro is such a digital service. The purchase dialog collects this consent before Stripe Checkout starts. The time of consent is stored on the Stripe checkout and on the subscription.
